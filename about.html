About Me

Who I Am

I completed my PhD in the Department of Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi in July 2025, specialising in theoretical quantum condensed matter physics. My doctoral work focused on the rich and rapidly growing field of twistronics — the physics of stacked two-dimensional materials rotated at precise angles relative to each other.

My research combined analytical theory with numerical computation. I studied Van der Waals heterostructures and Moiré materials, deriving effective continuum models and uncovering emergent phenomena such as flat bands, unconventional superconductivity, and fractal structures in twisted graphene systems. My work on Moiré fractals was published in Physical Review B and selected as an Editor's Suggestion.

Beyond my primary research, I have contributed to collaborative work on the optical properties of twisted bilayer graphene and have authored a broad topical review of the field, published in the Journal of Physics: Condensed Matter.
