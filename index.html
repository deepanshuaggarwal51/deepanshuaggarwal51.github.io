Research Focus Areas

Moiré Physics

Studying the emergent phenomena that arise in van der Waals systems with a slight twist angle, including flat bands, correlated insulator states, and unconventional superconductivity.

Van der Waals Systems

Investigating layered two-dimensional materials — graphene, hBN, and transition metal dichalcogenides — and their heterostructures, exploring band structures and quantum transport.

Fractal Quantum Systems

Discovering that sequences of Moiré-periodic potentials give rise to fractal-like hierarchical structures — Moiré fractals — with connections to Central Place Theory in economic geography.

Computational Methods

Developing numerical codes from scratch in Python, MATLAB, and Mathematica to compute band structures, optical matrix elements, Green's functions, and correlation functions.
